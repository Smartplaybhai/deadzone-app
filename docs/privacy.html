DEAD ZONE: LAST ARENA

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

DEAD ZONE: Last Arena ("the Game") is an offline single-player 3D survival shooter developed by Abdullah ("the Developer"). This policy explains what information the Game handles.

1. Information we do not collect

The Developer does not collect, store, sell or share any personal information. The Game has no account system, no online servers, and does not ask for your name, email, phone number, location, contacts, photos or microphone.

2. Data stored on your device

Your progress (coins, level, XP, unlocked items, settings and best scores) is saved only on your own device, in local app storage. It never leaves your device. Uninstalling the Game or clearing its data deletes it.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game shows ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use device identifiers such as the advertising ID, IP address and general device information to serve and measure ads and prevent fraud, as described in Google's Advertising Policy and Google's Privacy Policy. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads). Rewarded ads are always optional.

4. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13 and the Developer does not knowingly collect information from children. The Game contains cartoon-style zombie violence.

5. Permissions

The Game only needs internet access so that ads can load. It does not use any other sensitive permission.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with an updated date.
